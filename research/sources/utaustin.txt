L2258@P54: A.5.1 Router (Portable)
L2259@P54: 1. Wear a face shield when operating the router.

L2262@P54: 4. Do not stand the router on the cutter end when not in use.

L2273@P55: A.5.3 Portable Belt Sander
L2274@P55: 1. Place the sander on its side before plugging the power plug into the outlet
L2275@P55: 2. Securely clamp the work piece before sanding.
L2276@P55: 3. Start the sander before touching it to the surface to be sanded.
L2277@P55: 4. Disconnect the power plug before changing the sanding belt.
L2278@P55: 5. The weight of the sander will apply adequate pressure to the sanding surface in most cases. Do
L2279@P55: not apply pressure that causes the sander to slow down.
L2280@P55: A.5.4 Disk / Belt Sander
L2281@P55: 1. Check the belt or disk to make sure it is in good condition and not torn. The shop supervisor will
L2282@P55: replace worn belts or disks.
L2283@P55: 2. Keep fingers and hands clear of the moving or rotating surface.
L2284@P55: 3. Hold the work piece securely and use only moderate pressure.
L2285@P55: 4. Sand only on the downward motion side of the disk sander.
L2286@P55: 5. Move the work piece side to side on the sanding surface to prevent rapid wear of the belt or disc.
L2287@P55: A.5.5 Table and Radial Circular Saw
L2288@P55: 1. You must ask the supervisor's permission to use the radial arm saw or the table saw.
L2289@P55: 2. Unplug the machine before handling or changing the blade.
L2290@P55: 3. Select the proper blade for the cut to be made. Check the blade to be free of cracks or nicks, and
L2291@P55: that it is sharp.
L2292@P55: 4. Limit the blade extension to ~ inch through the piece being sawed.
L2293@P55: 5. Use the ripping fence or the cutoff gauge when cutting material.
L2294@P55: 6. Keep a push stick immediately available and use it to keep fingers away from the saw blade.
L2295@P55: 7. Feed the work piece at a moderate rate, but not so fast the motor slows down.
L2296@P55: 8. When using the fence to rip cut a work piece the operator and all observers must not stand in line
L2297@P55: with the work piece because it can get pinched between the spinning blade and the fence causing
L2298@P55: it to kick back (be fired strait back out of the saw at a very rapid rate).
L2299@P55: 9. When cutting large or long pieces on the table saw, use an assistant to SUPPORT the edge or end
L2300@P55: of large or long pieces being sawed. The assistant does not "feed" the material into or pull it
L2301@P55: through the saw. This can cause the operator to loose their balance if the work piece moves more
L2302@P55: rapidly then they anticipated and the operator can fall into the saw.
L2303@P55: 10. Make sure the table saw has a blade guard, splitter, and anti-kickback device installed and
L2304@P55: operational before using the saw. Exceptions may be made for specialty cuts (e.g., dados). Check
L2305@P55: with the shop supervisor before disengaging or removing these guards.
L2306@P55: 11. You may not cut any work piece on the radial arm saw that is less than 12 inches in length.
L2307@P55: 12. The table and radial arm saws are for cutting wood materials only.

L2352@P58: Appendix B: Student Shop After Hours Policy
L2353@P58: B.1 Rules for Student Machine Shop for after hours and weekends
L2354@P58: 1. All shop safety rules will be strictly adhered to, see: Appendix A.
L2355@P58: 2. Only physics approved students, working as part of an official physics department group, are
L2356@P58: allowed in the shop. Each individual must provide, on the sign-up sheet, his or her name and an
L2357@P58: emergency contact (this will include the name, relationship, and phone number of a responsible
L2358@P58: party in the event of an accident).
L2359@P58: 3. No machinery will be operated and no work will be done unless there are two student members
L2360@P58: physically present in the shop. This will allow, in the event of an injury, for the second person to
L2361@P58: call for help by using the EMERGENCY PHONE and then render aid. See: EMERGENCY
L2362@P58: PROCEDURES, below.
L2363@P58: 4. Any breach of safety procedures will result in the loss of shop privileges.
L2364@P58: 5. Faculty sponsor must approve after hours work and provide a means of contact for the group.
L2365@P58: 6. Leave doors open while working in student machine shop.
L2366@P58: B.2 Minor Emergencies
L2367@P58: If treatment is required for minor accidents, University Health Services or any ERT can be contacted. Telephone
L2368@P58: numbers and locations will be posted on the door by sign-in sheet.

L2372@P58: under the large RED sign. This will put you in direct contact with the University of Texas Police

L2374@P58: the room number (3.210/student shop).
L2375@P58: 2. Explain the nature and severity of the emergency and if Emergency Medical Services (EMS)

L2378@P58: the sink.
